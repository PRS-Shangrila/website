How this website handles information.

  • This website does not collect personal information. It has no forms, no accounts, no cookies, and no analytics or advertising trackers.
  • If you choose the light or dark theme, your choice is saved in your own browser only. It is never sent anywhere.
  • If you call, send a WhatsApp message or write to us, we receive the details you choose to share, such as your name, number and enquiry, and use them to reply. Phone, WhatsApp and email services handle messages under their own privacy policies.
  • This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may record technical information such as IP addresses in its server logs, under GitHub's own policy.
  • We do not publish names, phone numbers, vehicle numbers or photographs of individual residents.

Questions about this notice: please use the details on the Contact page.
