The building at a glance

    Why live here? എന്തുകൊണ്ട് ഇവിടെ?

    PRS Shangrila stands about 300 metres from Mahatma Gandhi Road. As you go from Pulimood Junction (GPO) towards Vanchiyoor, it is by the side of this road near Kunnumpuram Junction (Old GPO).

    It is an 11-storey building with car parking in the basement and on the ground floor. The main entrance lobby is on the ground floor, and there are 25 apartments on the 9 floors above it.

    Common facilities include an association room, a fitness centre, intercom, a security alert system, a children's park and landscaped open yards.
